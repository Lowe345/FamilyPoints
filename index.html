🔄 Start New Week?

This will save the current week to history and reset all kids to their starting points.

🗑️ Wipe History?

This will permanently delete all previous weeks. The current week will be kept. This cannot be undone.

⚠️ Are you absolutely sure?

All historical week data will be permanently deleted. There is no way to recover this. Are you certain you want to proceed?

💥 Reset Everything?

This will delete all data — every week, every log entry, all settings — and reset the app to its defaults. This cannot be undone.

⚠️ Final warning

Every piece of data will be permanently erased. Kids, settings, all history. The app will restart fresh. You cannot undo this.
